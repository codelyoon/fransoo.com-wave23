02 — Where it fits

The missing step in local marketing.

Most local marketing tries to get found: Google Business Profile, photos, posts, a decent site. Reviews are the part that compounds after someone has already walked in.

Wave23 doesn’t replace ads or a website. It catches the customer who is already standing in front of you and makes the review easy enough that they actually leave one. More reviews, more recent reviews, and a stronger local listing without buying another click.

They find you Search, maps, a referral. The listing has to look alive.

They tap The card opens the review. The listing gets proof for the next person.

03 — Why reviews

Google reviews do the selling when you’re not in the room.

Local ranking

Review count, recency, and rating are signals Google uses when it decides who shows up in Maps and local search.

Trust before the visit

People compare listings. A business with fresh reviews looks open, busy, and less of a risk than one with three reviews from 2022.

Higher intent clicks

A stronger listing gets more taps on call, directions, and website. That’s traffic you don’t pay for per click.

Social proof in the reply

Reviews answer the questions ads don’t: was it clean, on time, worth it, would they go back.

A feedback loop

Public reviews tell you what to fix. Private complaints are useful. Silent customers are not.

Compounds

One review helps a little. A steady stream changes how the business looks every month after that.
